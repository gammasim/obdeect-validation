Illuminators ILLN-01 and ILLS-01 are compared with xyzls at three distances (`sources.illuminator-*`). Arrival times are checked against the exact bent-ray integral; the xyzls time at grazing angles is recorded only (its refraction term is wrong for small |cz|). The night-sky camera photoelectrons are compared with the analytic expectation (`sources.night-sky-camera`).
