Validation of the curved and spherical atmosphere of obdeect propagation: analytic and independent-reference cases (Chapman extinction, spherical geometry, a Bouguer ray tracer for refraction, input guards), and cases on CORSIKA CURVED showers (curved flag in the stream, emission geometry, curved extinction, high-zenith chain against sim_telarray with its plane-parallel atmosphere). The new suite `curvature` selects them; the shower inputs are generated with `python -m obdeect_validation.adapters.curved_showers run`.
